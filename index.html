About Us

15+ Years of Nurturing Young Minds

Excel Abacus is an educational organisation dedicated to helping children develop strong mental abilities through Abacus and Mental Maths training.

With more than 15 years of experience, we conduct structured Abacus classes with a focus on concentration, memory, calculation ability, confidence and overall learning skills.

Our programs are conducted mainly through school-based classes, including academic-period and after-school programs.
